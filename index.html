About Us

Credit Printing & Paper Products Limited. established in 2010. Our company provides a one-stop service for printing and manufacturing. The products include color boxes, gift boxes, paper bags, display boxes, instruction manuals, labels, and hang tags. Our office is in Hong Kong, and our factory is in Dongguan, China. Our factory has obtained FSC and ISO certifications. Welcome to contact us for inquiries and quotations.
